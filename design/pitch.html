The problem

Uncollateralised credit against an anonymous wallet cannot work.

01

A limit inferred from wallet history is a limit anyone can manufacture.

02

One person opens unlimited wallets and draws unlimited credit from each.

03

Default costs the borrower nothing but a discarded keypair.

04

The only safe design left is collateral. A loan secured by collateral is a pawnshop, not BNPL.

Why Cleanverse is load-bearing

The honest test of an integration is what happens when you remove it.

A-Pass is bank-verified, wallet-bound, non-transferable and revocable. That is what lets a credential carry the risk collateral otherwise has to.

Remove Cleanverse from Kudira and you do not get a degraded product. You get nothing.

How it works

Five stages, one purchase.

Verify

Read the buyer's A-Pass. Standing only, never documents.

Underwrite

subTier sets the credit grade and limit, on-chain.

Settle

Merchant paid in full, in the same transaction.

Repay

Auto-debit pulls each installment as it falls due.

Enforce

On-time raises the grade, default lowers it.

The merchant carries no credit risk and waits for nothing. Kudira holds the risk, priced against a credential a borrower cannot fake or discard.

Integration surface

Four Cleanverse modules, each load-bearing.

A-Pass read · underwrite

query_apass gives tier, subTier, status and expiry. No plan may outlive the credential, enforced in the contract.

A-Pass write · grade

Repayment writes back through generate_apass. On-time +5, default −20, both saturating.

CCP compliance pool

KudiraPool integrates the Cleanverse Compliance Protocol as a lending pool. Live and discriminating: validator/verify, and its on-chain twin complianceVerify on the CCP validator, both return true for a credentialed wallet, false for one generated a second ago.

CVA settlement

Both parties must hold a credential, enforced on-chain. We settle in KUSDC, a CVA (Cleanverse Verified Asset) we issued through Cleanverse's documented /atoken/launch path after the Base faucet stopped dispensing aUSDC on 24 July. Same min_tier 5 rule, gated identically. We can mint KUSDC but cannot mint past a credential check.

What we found

Four things a live sandbox taught us that no local test could.

The one-second window

Auto-debit could never earn its reward. A payment was on time only at the exact due second, which a chain cannot hit. The tests were green against a moment that cannot happen. Fixed with a grace period, stored per plan.

The invisible aUSDC gate

Nothing in the token's ABI shows it gates transfers. The check lives in _update behind namespaced storage, reverting NoAPass before the balance check. Reading the interface gives a confident, wrong answer.

The TLS handshake

Node 24's default post-quantum key share is silently dropped by the Cleanverse edge. Every request from Node timed out while curl succeeded. Restricting to classical curves fixes it and still negotiates TLS 1.3.

The Travel Rule boundary

TR_001 for our tx, CV_100 for another institution's wallet. That contrast isolates the cause to indexing: our CVA sits outside the settlement flow Travel Rule reports from.
