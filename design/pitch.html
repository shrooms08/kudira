The problem

Every on-chain lender is a pawnshop.

Anonymous wallets have nothing to lose, so DeFi lending converged on the only model that works without identity: make the borrower post more than they take.

Traditional DeFi lending

OVERCOLLATERALIZED
Deposit $150 of collateral. Borrow $100. If you disappear, the protocol liquidates and is made whole.
Works without identity

Buy now, pay later

UNCOLLATERALIZED BY DEFINITION
Receive $100 of goods today. Promise to pay in four installments. If you disappear, the lender eats the loss.
Impossible without identity
Overcollateralization is not a design choice. It is what is left when you cannot identify your borrower.
Why Cleanverse is load-bearing

Take Cleanverse out and
there is no product.

Without verified identity

Credit limit has to be inferred from wallet history, which any borrower can manufacture.
One person opens unlimited wallets and draws unlimited credit.
Default costs the borrower nothing but a discarded keypair.
The only safe design left is collateral, and a loan secured by collateral is a pawnshop, not BNPL.

With CVI

The credential is bank-verified, wallet-bound, non-transferable and revocable.
One person, one credential, one credit line across every merchant.
Default is recorded against an identity the borrower cannot discard.
Identity carries the risk that collateral otherwise has to.
Removing CVI does not degrade Kudira. It deletes it.
How it works

One checkout. Five moving parts.

1

Verify

Buyer connects a wallet holding a CVI credential. No credential, no credit line.
2

Underwrite

Credential standing sets the limit and the installment schedule.
3

Settle

Merchant is paid in full in the same transaction as origination.
4

Repay

Installments auto-debit on their due date. On-time payment raises the grade.
5

Enforce

Default lowers the grade. The credential stays active so repayment is always possible.
The merchant takes no credit risk and waits for nothing. The buyer's credential carries the risk instead of a deposit they do not have.

Integration surface

Where the stack does the work.

CVI

The underwriting input

Credential standing sets the credit limit. Repayment behaviour writes back to subTier through generate_apass, so the grade is published to the network, not held privately.
CVA

The settlement asset

We settle in KUSDC, a CVA issued under our Issue Member scope via Launch CVA, after Cleanverse's Base faucet stopped dispensing on 24 July. Same min_tier 5 rule, gated identically on both sides of every transfer.
CCP

The compliance gate

KudiraPool is a Pattern 2 single-contract lending pool registered with the CVI Compliance Validator. complianceVerify(pool, wallet) is read on-chain alongside the REST check, and the merchant dashboard shows both, live.
Rules

Policy, not decoration

Our RuleV2 lives in Cleanverse's validator and in the contract's own mirror. Origination checks both. If they ever disagree, the UI says so rather than picking one.
We can mint KUSDC. We cannot mint past a credential check. The gate is Cleanverse's, not ours.
What we found

Four things that only surface
on a live chain.

The interval with no width

Auto-debit fires when an installment falls due, but "on time" meant paid before the due date. The intersection was a single instant, so the reward could never fire. Our tests were green because vm.warp landed exactly on it.

The gate the ABI hides

CVA transfers check both parties inside _update, behind ERC-7201 storage, before the balance check. Nothing in the interface says so. Reading the ABI gives a confident wrong answer.

A handshake that never completes

Node 24's default TLS group list leads with a post-quantum key share. The sandbox edge drops those hellos silently: TCP connects, then nothing. Any classical group works.

Where Travel Rule ends

Reporting is bound to the indexed settlement flow. TR_001 for our transfer, CV_100 for another institution's wallet, which isolates the cause to indexing rather than permissions. The merchant panel shows the real failure and explains it.
Each of these was found by running the thing, not by reading about it.
Pilot path

Who signs the first contract.

Cleanverse asks whether a project can be piloted with institutions or merchants. Three routes, ordered by how quickly they close.
1

Merchant acquirers

FASTEST
Processors already integrated with Cleanverse can offer Kudira at checkout to their existing merchant base. Distribution without a new sales motion.
2

Licensed lenders

HIGHEST VOLUME
Consumer credit firms supply the liquidity and use CVI for the affordability checks they are already legally required to perform.
3

Emerging market fintechs

LARGEST UPSIDE
Philippines, Brazil, Mexico, Peru and Malaysia are Cleanverse ramp countries where thin credit files block BNPL entirely and a portable bank-verified credential is the only viable signal. The demo is set in Manila.
A credible route to a pilot, not a claimed one.
